About

I am a Full Professor and Ph.D. Supervisor with the School of Software at Dalian University of Technology. My research focuses on contactless wireless sensing, underwater wireless systems, wireless rechargeable sensor networks, pervasive computing, and intelligent IoT systems.

Research Interests

Contactless Wireless Sensing

WiFi, mmWave and UWB sensing for activity, motion, health and environmental perception.

Underwater Wireless Systems

Visible-light communication, underwater positioning, optical tags and underwater sensing.

Wireless Charging & Energy Networks

Wireless rechargeable sensor networks, charging scheduling, security and efficiency.

Smart Health & Intelligent IoT

Non-contact health monitoring, multimodal sensing and intelligent IoT applications.
