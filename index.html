About

I am a Full Professor and Ph.D. Supervisor with the School of Software at Dalian University of Technology. My research centers on wireless sensing and intelligent IoT systems, spanning contactless sensing, underwater wireless systems, wireless rechargeable sensor networks, and human-centered intelligent sensing.

Research Interests

Contactless Wireless Sensing

WiFi, mmWave and UWB sensing for human activity, motion, health and environmental perception.

Underwater Wireless Systems

Visible-light communication, underwater positioning, optical tags and underwater sensing systems.

Intelligent IoT

Energy-efficient IoT, cyber-physical systems, edge intelligence and intelligent sensing infrastructures.

Smart Health & AI

Non-contact health monitoring, activity recognition, multimodal sensing and data-driven intelligent systems.
