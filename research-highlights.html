Research Highlights

Selected research systems and technical directions in wireless sensing, underwater wireless systems, intelligent IoT, wireless charging, and human-centered sensing.

3 × ACM MobiComWiFi · mmWave · UWBUnderwater Optical SystemsIntelligent IoT

Rather than listing publications alone, this page highlights representative research threads, the technical problem each work addresses, and the broader systems direction behind it.

HIGHLIGHT 01ACM MobiCom 2024

UWBeacon: Centimeter-Level Underwater Positioning

UWBeacon explores high-precision underwater positioning and develops a practical system for centimeter-level localization in challenging underwater environments.

  • Underwater positioning with visible-light based sensing and communication.
  • System-oriented design for practical underwater deployment.
  • Representative work in the lab's underwater intelligent sensing line.
Underwater Positioning · Optical Sensing
HIGHLIGHT 02ACM MobiCom 2024

Fine-grained Textile Moisture Sensing with Commodity UWB

This work turns commodity UWB radio into a fine-grained sensing platform for textile moisture, demonstrating how wireless signals can perceive subtle material and environmental changes.

  • Commodity UWB-based fine-grained sensing.
  • Contactless material and environmental perception.
  • Extends RF sensing beyond conventional human activity recognition.
UWB Sensing · Environmental Perception
HIGHLIGHT 03ACM MobiCom 2021

Shrimp: Robust Underwater Visible Light Communication

Shrimp develops a robust underwater visible-light communication system, addressing the reliability challenges of optical links in practical underwater environments.

  • Robust underwater visible-light communication.
  • Practical system implementation and experimental validation.
  • Foundation for later underwater communication and sensing systems.
Underwater Communication · VLC
HIGHLIGHT 04IEEE TMC / ICDCS

WiFi-based Contactless Human Sensing

A sequence of works investigates device-free sensing using commodity WiFi, including handwriting recognition, instantaneous angular-speed measurement, authentication, and activity-related perception.

  • WiWrite: device-free handwriting recognition with COTS WiFi.
  • Wi-Rotate: instantaneous angular-speed measurement using WiFi signals.
  • Contactless authentication and activity sensing with WiFi CSI.
WiFi CSI · Contactless Sensing
HIGHLIGHT 05IEEE TMC 2024

AirWrite: Aerial Handwriting with mmWave

AirWrite uses millimeter-wave sensing to track aerial handwriting trajectories and recognize written content, showing the potential of mmWave for fine-grained human-computer interaction.

  • Fine-grained motion trajectory sensing.
  • mmWave-based contactless interaction.
  • Integration of signal processing and intelligent recognition.
mmWave · Human–Computer Interaction
HIGHLIGHT 06IEEE TMC · IEEE/ACM ToN · INFOCOM

Wireless Rechargeable Sensor Networks

A long-term research program studies reliable, efficient, secure and three-dimensional wireless charging for IoT and sensor networks, covering scheduling, Fresnel-zone effects, privacy and robustness.

  • 3-D and directional wireless charging scheduling.
  • Fresnel-zone aware charging and through-wall charging.
  • Security, privacy and robustness of rechargeable sensor networks.
Wireless Power · IoT · Optimization
